# Student Guide: Wi-Fi Data Gathering with WiGLE

Welcome to the School Wi-Fi Mapping Project! In this activity, you will become a **wardriver**—someone who scans and logs wireless signals while moving. Your data will be combined with your classmates' to help map Wi-Fi coverage, find "dead zones," and identify wireless interference on campus.

---

## 📱 Step 1: Install the WiGLE App
WiGLE (Wireless Geographic Logging Engine) is a free app used to scan and record Wi-Fi networks, Bluetooth devices, and cell towers.

*   **Android Devices (Highly Recommended):** 
    1. Open the **Google Play Store**.
    2. Search for **WiGLE Wi-Fi Wardriving** and install it.
*   **iOS Devices (iPhone/iPad):**
    1. Open the **Apple App Store**.
    2. Search for **WiGLE Wi-Fi** and install it.
    3. > [!NOTE]
       > **Apple iOS Restriction:** Due to Apple’s security and privacy rules, iOS apps are *not* allowed to access raw Wi-Fi scan details (SSID or signal strength). If you are using an iPhone, you will only scan cell towers and Bluetooth devices. **Don't worry!** You can still participate by:
       > * Pairing up with a classmate who has an Android device.
       > * Mapping Bluetooth and cell coverage.
       > * Acting as the team navigator or data manager.

---

## ⚙️ Step 2: Configure App Permissions & Settings
To scan effectively, WiGLE needs access to your device's hardware.

1.  **Grant Permissions:** When you first open the app, it will ask for **Location/GPS** and **Physical Activity / Device scanning** permissions. Choose **"Allow all the time"** or **"While using the app."**
2.  **Turn GPS and Wi-Fi/Bluetooth ON:** Make sure your phone's GPS (Location services), Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth are all turned on in your system settings.
3.  **Disable Battery Optimization (Android only):** 
    *   Many Android phones aggressively shut down Wi-Fi scanning when the screen turns off to save battery.
    *   If possible, go to your phone's battery settings and set WiGLE to **"Don't Optimize"** or **"Unrestricted."**

---

## 🏃 Step 3: Mapping the Campus (Data Gathering)
To map the school accurately, your class will split into teams. Each team will cover a different area of the campus.

### How to Scan:
1.  **Start the Scan:** Open the WiGLE app and make sure the scan toggle is active (you will see networks popping up on the list and map screens inside the app).
2.  **Walk Slowly:** Walk at a normal, steady pace. If you run, your phone won't have enough time to scan for networks and record your GPS position.
3.  **Hold Your Phone Correctly:** Hold your phone in front of you. Try not to cover the top or back of the phone with your hands, as this is where the internal Wi-Fi antennas are located.
4.  **Cover the Area:** Walk up and down hallways, step into classrooms if permitted, and cover outdoor spaces like courtyards or play fields. 
5.  **Watch the GPS Accuracy:** In the app, check the GPS status. If you go deep inside concrete buildings, your GPS signal might fade. Make a note of areas where your map location stops updating.

---

## 📤 Step 4: Exporting Your Data
Once your scan is finished, you need to export the log file.

1.  **Stop Scanning:** Toggle the scanning switch to OFF in the WiGLE app.
2.  **Export CSV:**
    *   **Android:** Go to the app menu (three dots or slide-out menu) -> Tap **"Database"** -> Tap **"Export CSV"** or **"Export to SD Card."**
    *   **iOS:** Go to **"Uploads"** -> Tap the share icon next to your recent run -> Choose **"Export CSV."**
3.  **Send the File:** 
    *   Transfer the exported `.csv` file to your computer or your teacher's computer. 
    *   You can do this via **Google Drive**, **Email**, **Slack/Teams**, or **AirDrop**.
    *   *Tip:* WiGLE CSV filenames usually look like: `WigleWifi_2026xxxxxxxx.csv`.

---

## 🗺️ Step 5: Uploading to the School Mapper
1. Open the **Wiggle Mapper** webpage on your computer.
2. Drag and drop your `.csv` file directly onto the map screen (you can upload multiple files at once!).
3. Select your school's Wi-Fi network from the list to see your coverage map!
